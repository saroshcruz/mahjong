Our Story

A Game Born
at the Front

Vineeta discovered mahjong as an army wife, stationed far from home. Around a table with fellow army wives, tiles became a language of patience, strategy and belonging.

She brought that game home to India. She taught it to her children, her neighbours, her community. And she saw what the table did: it made strangers into friends, and friends into family.

The Indian Mahjong Association was born from that conviction: that this game, in all its richness, belongs in every Indian home.

— Vineeta Sahni
Our Principles

What Makes IMA Different

The Authentic Game

We play and teach the true Chinese mahjong tradition: 144 tiles, proper scoring and the complete ruleset as it was meant to be played.

An Indian Touch

Adapted with warmth for Indian families: workshops in Hindi and English, family-friendly sessions and a community that feels like home.

Open to All

Every gender, age and background is welcome at our table. From eight to eighty, from beginner to champion. The tiles make no distinction.
